# Primary sources and attribution

The paper's bibliography and the replacement prior-work section give the complete references. proofs/SOURCE_LEDGER_FINAL.md records the direct examination of Gunji, Rück, Semaev and the relevant book passages. history/normalization_repair/SOURCE_AUDIT_UPDATE.md records the earlier Miller, Harvey, Cassels, Voloch, Galbraith and Schoof checks; its then-unread-source statuses are superseded by the final ledger.

Miller's original manuscript is dated 6 May 1986. It describes straight-line programs for rational functions with prescribed divisors. The 2004 publication supplies the standard divisor, addition and composition identities in Section 4.1, especially equations (7)–(9). In particular, the composition identity used in the uniform-order proof is classical, not an additional novelty claim. Semaev (1998) and Rück (1999) precede that published exposition; citation of the earlier manuscript avoids implying the opposite chronological dependence.
