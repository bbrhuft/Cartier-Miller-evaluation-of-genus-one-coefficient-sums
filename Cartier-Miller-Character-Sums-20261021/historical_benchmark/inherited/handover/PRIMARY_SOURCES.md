# Primary-source starting register

This register identifies sources to read, not a completed novelty audit. URLs can fail or serve later versions; record the actual version and pages obtained. Look for authorized journal archives, author copies and institutional repositories. Do not treat a failed download or a search gap as evidence that a result does not exist.

## Closest constructions

Voloch, J. F. (1990). Explicit p-descent for elliptic curves in characteristic p. Compositio Mathematica, 74(3), 247–258. Primary archive: https://www.numdam.org/item/CM_1990__74_3_247_0/ . Inspect the high-part polynomial and its normalization. Cross-check the later correction of scale and sign in the 1997 paper; the project reports such a correction, which this task must verify.

Voloch, J. F. (1997). An analogue of the Weierstrass zeta-function in characteristic p. Acta Arithmetica, 79(1), 1–6. DOI: https://doi.org/10.4064/aa-79-1-1-6 . Archive candidate: https://matwbn.icm.edu.pl/ksiazki/aa/aa79/aa7911.pdf . Inspect quasi-periods, logarithmic derivatives, polynomial-power formulas and differential conventions, and trace any relevant identities to Cassels, Deuring and Gunji.

Voloch, J. F. (undated revised preprint). The discrete logarithm problem on elliptic curves and descents. Author copy: https://www.math.canterbury.ac.nz/~f.voloch/Pdfs/disclog3.pdf . Pages 3–4 are a key starting point, but read the definitions. It states a high-part map yM(x) in the anomalous setting and compares it with Semaev’s transfer. Determine exactly what scale is fixed and which part of the weighted-sum problem follows. Do not silently extend a trace-one statement to all traces or nonsquare fibres.

Semaev, I. A. (1998). Evaluation of discrete logarithms in a group of p-torsion points of an elliptic curve in characteristic p. Mathematics of Computation, 67(221), 353–356. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1090/S0025-5718-98-00887-4 . Journal PDF candidate: https://www.ams.org/mcom/1998-67-221/S0025-5718-98-00887-4/S0025-5718-98-00887-4.pdf . Inspect the differentiated recurrence, field-operation model, divisor conventions and conversion to the scalar required here. The journal endpoint previously returned 403; find an authorized alternative if necessary.

Rück, H.-G. (1999). On the discrete logarithm in the divisor class group of curves. Mathematics of Computation, 68(226), 805–806. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1090/S0025-5718-99-01043-1 . Check the scope of the divisor-class transfer and whether it supplies an existing route after a normalization adapter.

Miller, V. S. (1986). Short Programs for functions on Curves. Author-hosted report: https://crypto.stanford.edu/miller/miller.pdf . This is a 1986 report, not the 2004 journal article. Inspect the short-program complexity and avoidance of expanded functions. Attribute the recurrence machinery accurately.

Miller, V. S. (2004). The Weil pairing, and its efficient calculation. Journal of Cryptology, 17(4), 235–261. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1007/s00145-004-0315-8 . Distinguish this publication from the earlier report.

## Generic coefficient algorithms and preparation

Bostan, A., Gaudry, P., & Schost, É. (2007). Linear recurrences with polynomial coefficients and application to integer factorization and Cartier–Manin operator. SIAM Journal on Computing, 36(6), 1777–1806. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1137/S0097539704443793 . Author copy: https://mathexp.eu/bostan/publications/BoGaSc07.pdf . Inspect the exact transition-matrix, unit and denominator requirements for the adapter in COMPARATOR_MATHEMATICS.md. The paper’s published coefficient applications do not automatically prove our complete adapter.

Schoof, R. (1985). Elliptic curves over finite fields and the computation of square roots mod p. Mathematics of Computation, 44(170), 483–494. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1090/S0025-5718-1985-0777280-6 . This establishes preparation, not the weighted output by itself. Distinguish conservative manuscript bounds from improved analyses.

Schoof, R. (1995). Counting points on elliptic curves over finite fields. Journal de Théorie des Nombres de Bordeaux, 7(1), 219–254. DOI: https://doi.org/10.5802/jtnb.142 . Archive: https://www.numdam.org/item/JTNB_1995__7_1_219_0/ . Inspect which practical point-counting assumptions apply to the chosen trace provider.

PARI/GP official documentation, Elliptic curves: https://pari.math.u-bordeaux.fr/dochtml/ref-stable/Elliptic_curves.html . Record the exact installed version and function behaviour. A quartic Jacobian adapter must be checked against the corresponding genus-one model and sign, not only documented as calling ellap.

FLINT official documentation: https://flintlib.org/doc/ ; python-flint official documentation: https://python-flint.readthedocs.io/ . Identify the actual polynomial, matrix, modular-arithmetic and multipoint kernels used. Native library use alone does not establish which asymptotic algorithm ran.

## Corrections and polynomial arithmetic

Giorgi, P., Grenet, B., & Roche, D. S. (2020). Fast in-place algorithms for polynomial operations: Division, evaluation, interpolation. ISSAC 2020, 210–217. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1145/3373207.3404061 . Author copy: https://membres-ljk.imag.fr/Bruno.Grenet/publis/GGR20.pdf ; preprint: https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.10304 . The product-tree and interpolation mechanisms are classical; inspect its earlier references for original attribution. Do not describe the application’s fast finite-part corrections as new general polynomial algorithms.

Montgomery, P. L. (1987). Speeding the Pollard and elliptic curve methods of factorization. Mathematics of Computation, 48(177), 243–264. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1090/S0025-5718-1987-0866113-7 . Check simultaneous inversion and distinguish implemented per-query batching from proposed lockstep batching.

## Ledger requirements

For each claim retain a primary URL/DOI, author/year, version, theorem or page, hypotheses, precise output, cost model, preparation and limitations. Save source hashes for downloaded versions where feasible. Short compliant quotations may clarify a normalization convention; use paraphrases and exact page attribution for the main discussion.

The initial register omits many potential adjacent sources. Follow the mathematics to relevant third-kind Cartier, generalized-Jacobian, descent and cohomology algorithms. Make neither novelty nor nonexistence claims merely because a method is absent from this list.
