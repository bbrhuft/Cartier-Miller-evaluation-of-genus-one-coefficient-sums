# Comparison matrix: prior constructions versus the Cartier–Miller weighted-sum evaluation

Target scalar throughout: C_f(a) = Σ_{x≠a} χ(f(x))/(x − a) ∈ F_p, together with its multi-pole and affine-point variants (Theorem A3). Equivalently, by (A1), the high part H(a) = Σ_{m≥p} c_m a^{m−p} of f^h.

Classification key: **E** exact prior algorithm for this scalar (after at most a trivial relabelling) · **A** provable consequence of a prior result with an explicit adapter derived here · **S** special-case overlap · **J** adjacent (different output or workload) · **U** unresolved.
