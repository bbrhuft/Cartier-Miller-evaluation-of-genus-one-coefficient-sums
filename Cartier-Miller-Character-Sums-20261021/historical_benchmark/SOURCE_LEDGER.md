# Source ledger — Cartier–Miller prior-work audit

Prepared 20–21 October 2026. Each row records what was actually obtained and read, the version, the page or statement, and the exact claim it supports. **How sources were read.** Publisher and preprint servers were not reachable from the shell (proxy HTTP 403). Every paper below was read through a web-fetch tool that extracts the PDF text and answers targeted questions. Full verbatim transcription was refused, so the ledger quotes only short formulas and phrases. Displays the extraction dropped are marked as such. Where a normalization mattered, it was then checked numerically. No downloaded PDF bytes were saved, so source-file hashes are unavailable; the URL and the printed version line are recorded instead. Kernel source files fetched from GitHub *were* saved and hashed (see the end of this ledger).

Status codes: **R** read (body text) · **P** partially read (named pages or sections only) · **A** abstract or metadata only · **N** not obtained in this audit (reason given).

## Mechanism searches (negative results are not evidence of novelty)

On 20–21 October 2026 the following web searches returned **no** source evaluating Σ χ(f(x))/(x − a) mod p, or the high part of f^{(p−1)/2} at a point, for curves of general trace. Each search returned adjacent or unrelated work only:

* "Cartier operator differential of the third kind finite field logarithmic derivative Miller function computation coefficient of f^((p−1)/2) evaluation at a point" (extended)
* "'Legendre symbol' sum '1/(x−a)' modulo p elliptic curve character sum field-valued truncated binomial 'f(x)^((p−1)/2)' high part polynomial evaluation" (extended)
* "equivalence of Semaev Smart Satoh-Araki anomalous elliptic curve attacks descent map logarithmic derivative" (extended). This found Vnd2, an arXiv/ePrint paper on the Smart attack and logarithmic derivatives (ePrint 2017/187; fetch refused by robots policy), and works on super-anomalous curves. **None was read.**

Unread candidates worth checking in a follow-up (ePrint 2017/187 = arXiv:1702.07107 was read later: not relevant, row GP17): "Cryptography, connections, cocycles and crystals: a p-adic exploration of the discrete logarithm problem"; Blake, Seroussi and Smart (1999, ch. V) and Galbraith (2012) as expositions of the anomalous attacks; and the Flajolet–Salvy selected-coefficient work credited by BGS07.
