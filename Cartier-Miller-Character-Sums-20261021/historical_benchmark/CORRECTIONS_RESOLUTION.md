## Gates resolved before freezing the benchmark candidate

The benchmark candidate is the **unchanged** inherited `cmcs.py` (SHA-256 763c8681…c59c) plus the separately frozen comparator package (FREEZE digest `09202ac1edab4f3e…28ac0`). The statement corrections G1–G6 change no frozen arithmetic. The protocol asked for corrections to be resolved before the freeze; that order was followed. The freeze record (local clock 2026-10-20T23:3x Z) postdates the K-family checks.
