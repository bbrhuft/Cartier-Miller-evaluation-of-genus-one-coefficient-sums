# Changelog

## 2026.10.12 (later) — ordinary Cartier-slope feasibility study

The ordinary Cartier-slope feasibility package, revision 2, was added unchanged as research/ordinary_k_feasibility_20261012/, with a RELEASE_NOTE.md. It derives Theorem A (Frobenius mod p in the low-pole basis is [[0, −β],[0, H]]) and Lemma B (κ ≡ σ_C/p for the canonical-subgroup kernel sum of any ordinary lift, pending specialist review), with further recovery formulas. It records that the canonical-lift, étale and Serre–Tate constructions examined give no cheaper complete algorithm for κ; no lower bound, speed-up or novelty is claimed. Its pilot and independently written checks, with failure injection, pass as recorded. The package's 13 `.log` evidence files were force-added despite the repository's `*.log` ignore rule, so that its own manifest verifies. README, STATUS, RESULTS, HUMAN_REVIEW, REPRODUCTION and PROVENANCE were updated. No theorem, recognizer or evaluator of the earlier releases changed.

The study can now be rerun from this repository alone. The three data files its scripts need from the Ordinary_K research handover were added under `handover_inputs/`, matching the handover's manifest; the rest of that handover is not redistributed. `research/cm_cartier_comparison_20261012/core/fixed_D_evaluator.py` now adds its own directory to `sys.path`, so that its `d7_seed` import works when another script loads it by path under `python -I`. Its arithmetic is unchanged, its fixed-discriminant suite still passes, and that package's SHA256SUMS.json entry was updated. Run from the repository, the pilot passed all six steps (eight of its twelve outputs byte-identical to the recorded ones) and the bounded independent profile passed 1,531 checks; outputs are in `repository_rerun_20261012/`.

The release manifest was also repaired. `verify_release.py` had been failing on `main`, for three reasons: README.md was edited after the manifest was written, `Walk the Volcano.png` had been added without an entry, and three review logs listed in the manifest (history/reviews/depth_three_b0_20261012/*.log) had never been committed because of the `*.log` ignore rule. The three logs were restored from the original Depth_Three_B0_Adversarial_Review_20261012.zip; their SHA-256 values equal the manifest's. They were force-added, and SHA256SUMS.json was regenerated over every file in the release.

## 2026.10.12 — B=0 depth-three family and the CM-to-Cartier package

The depth-three family below the B = 0 seed (discriminant −256, p = x² + 64y²) is now recognized; it was previously listed as deferred. It was merged from the 12 October handover onto the consolidated code. The module is core/depth_three_b0_recognize.py, with the adversarial review's schema patch applied. It is selected by `--family b0` and also covered by the combined recognizer. Its optional Las Vegas sign method has its own helper with no claimed success bound, because the A = 0 routine's 1/5 bound applies only to its own family. The validator, the exact derivation and B0_SEED_FAMILY_20261012.md were added. The consolidation validator now checks that the fixture's B = 0-chain curves are recognized, where it previously checked that they were not.

The CM-to-Cartier research package of 12 October, revision 1 after its adversarial review, was added as research/cm_cartier_comparison_20261012/. It contains the comparison β/H ≡ −(3B/2A)s₂, the re-derivation of the six family slopes, the D = −7 seed and evaluator, the D = −8 control and the fixed-discriminant table. Its validators now default to the release's own research code. Against the merged code all 13 steps of its suite pass, including the fixed-discriminant suite. That suite had failed 2,256 checks against the 10 October release, because the B = 0 recognizer was missing. verify_release.py gained B = 0 and D = −7 smoke checks. The records of both reviews and both handovers are under history/. The Ireland–Rosen chapter scan bundled in one handover, and the nested handover archive containing the manuscript and GPL code, were not redistributed.

## 2026.10.10 — deterministic trace sign and cost correction

The depth-three CM preparation now fixes the trace sign deterministically by Ireland and Rosen (1990, Ch. 18, Theorem 4) applied to the j = 0 seed. Only the cubic-nonresidue search for √−3 remains randomized. The consolidated zero-error Las Vegas test, with its 1/5 bound, is kept as `sign_method='las_vegas'`. The supplied update was merged onto the consolidated code rather than replacing it, so no consolidation repair was lost. A new note, DETERMINISTIC_TRACE_SIGN_20261010.md, records the rule, and a new independent PARI check passed on 2,075 curves. The depth-three validation outputs were regenerated for the new code.

The rounded-third cost statement was corrected. A classical ECCC report (Tal, TR26-211, unrefereed) claims randomized p^{1/2−δ} factorial algorithms for most primes, and a deterministic logarithmic-factor improvement for every prime. The equivalence with ⌊p/3⌋! mod p is unchanged, and no polylogarithmic method is known. The received update files are kept in history/handover_records/depth_three_update_20261010/.

## 2026.10.09 — first public release

This first public release of the Cartier–Miller extension research covers the branch formula, normalized rational 2-isogeny transport, direct recognition through depth two from the stated ordinary easy seeds, and one consolidated depth-three family with CM trace preparation. It also includes the rounded-third obstruction: for p ≡ 2 mod 3, the boundary a_(p+1)/3 equals 3/(2(⌊p/3⌋!)³) mod p, so the complete weighted sum there is equivalent to a Gauss factorial.

Preparing the release added a replacement validator for the closure branch, whose original validator was not retained. It fixed two absolute fixture paths in independent checks. It added an unchanged copy of `elliptic_prefix.py` under retained/, and README, RESULTS, STATUS, REPRODUCTION, PROVENANCE, the MIT LICENSE and CITATION.cff. Every validator was rerun in the release layout, including 2,640 new PARI trace cross-checks. GPL-licensed and paper material was excluded. No theorem, recognizer or evaluator changed.
