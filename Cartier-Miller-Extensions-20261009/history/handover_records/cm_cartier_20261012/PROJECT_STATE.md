The validated B=0 review checked 1,885,840 nonsingular models at twelve eligible primes through 617, exactly 16,448 accepted, plus 1,268 coefficient/point-count controls, 60 original-cubic branch checks and large trace-sign witnesses. These are finite implementation checks; the all-prime recognition argument rests on the exact algebra and integer exclusions. No new timing or novelty claim was made.

The proposed target for AB nonzero is beta/H = -(3B/(2A))s_2 after suitable ordinary CM reduction, s_2=E_2^* E_4/E_6. It must be proved or corrected. beta/H scales with the model; A beta/(BH) is the dimensionless expression. All scalings, embeddings, twists and integrality assumptions must be explicit.

Every ordinary curve over F_p has an imaginary-quadratic endomorphism algebra. That observation does not make fixed-small-discriminant precomputation a fast method for generic curves: finding or representing a growing CM order is a separate cost. Keep the proposed theorem restricted to a fixed, certified order or a clearly charged input representation.
